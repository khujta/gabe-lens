lab-04 · cosmos.gl as the layout engine @cosmos.gl/graph (MIT — never @cosmograph/cosmos, CC-BY-NC at the same version) runs the force simulation IN SHADERS with a real cluster force: every node is pinned to its entity's cluster (setPointClusters) whose centre is the adapter's anchor (setClusterPositions). Each tick getPointPositions() is harvested (2D; z = the sub-layer band) and the SAME instanced three renderer as lab-01 draws (three-view.js). Under swiftshader the "GPU" sim is CPU-emulated — its tick cost here is not the GPU's.
?ticks=N caps the simulation (default 120 — cosmos ticks once per ITS render frame, ~0.6/s under swiftshader) · cluster centres on a RING (a 2D anchor set; the adapter's x-line collapses a 2D force) · ?cluster=0.6 the cluster force · the purity column compares it with the bake and the d3 lane.
